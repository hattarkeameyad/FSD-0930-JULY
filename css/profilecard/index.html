Shinchan

Crayon Shin-chan is a famous Japanese manga and anime series created by Yoshito Usui. It follows Shinnosuke "Shin-chan" Nohara, a cheeky five-year-old boy living in Kasukabe, Saitama, Japan, known for his funny, unfiltered, and mischievous antics

  • Shinnosuke
    A goofy five-year-old who loves Chocobi snacks and Action Kamen, but often shocks adults with his cheeky behavior.
  • Hiroshi and Misae
    Shin-chan's hardworking parents who constantly deal with the chaos he causes..
  • Himawari:
    Shin-chan's baby sister.
  • Shiro
    The family's fluffy white dog.
  • Friends
    Kazama, Nene, Masao, and Bo, who form the Kasukabe Defense Organization.
